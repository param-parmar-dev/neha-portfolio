Banking Portfolio, Branch Forecasting & Capital Analysis

Managing financial visibility across a $40M+ portfolio and 350+ branch network — spanning two roles at the same institution — through liquidity monitoring, forecasting, reconciliation, and capital analysis.

Liquidity monitoring — OwnedBudget & forecasting — OwnedCost analysis — OwnedCapital analysis — Evaluated
Executive Summary

A regulated, high-volume banking environment required daily cash visibility, reliable account reconciliation, and consistent reporting across hundreds of branches. Budgets and forecasts had to support regional resource allocation while controlling operating costs, and capital decisions needed quantitative return analysis paired with qualitative risk judgment.

This case consolidates two roles at the same institution: Financial Analyst (2017–2021), analyzing performance across a 350+ branch network, and Finance Manager (2021–2023), owning liquidity monitoring and reporting for a $40M+ portfolio. Across both, I improved control accuracy by 30%, forecast accuracy by 15%, and branch operational efficiency by 25%.

01Business problem

Banking finance runs under continuous regulatory scrutiny and high transaction and reporting volume. Daily liquidity, branch-level performance, and capital allocation all had to be visible, reconciled, and defensible.

02My role

Liquidity monitoring — Owned (Finance Manager, 2021–2023). Budget & forecasting — Owned. Branch performance analysis — Owned (Financial Analyst, 2017–2021). Capital analysis — Evaluated.

03Approach & controls

Consistency at scale is the hard part: reconciling accounts and reporting performance reliably across hundreds of branches, while budgets and forecasts guided real regional resource allocation. Capital decisions demanded both a return number and a risk judgment — neither sufficient alone.

  • Oversaw daily cash-flow and liquidity monitoring for a $40M+ portfolio, improving control accuracy by 30% through structured monitoring processes.
  • Led annual budgets and rolling forecast cycles across operational departments, improving forecast accuracy by 15%.
  • Analyzed performance across 350+ branches using centralized reporting, improving operational efficiency by 25% through data-driven recommendations.
  • Conducted ROI and cost-benefit analysis on capital investments, improving long-term investment efficiency by 20%.

Daily liquidity and branch reporting were reconciled before use in forecasting or capital decisions; control quality was treated as part of the analysis. Capital proposals were evaluated using ROI, payback, cost-benefit analysis, and qualitative risk considerations — the recreated portfolio visual may display this as a risk-return matrix.

06What I'd improve next

  • Controls learned under regulatory pressure transfer everywhere; the discipline outlasts the environment.
  • Return without a risk judgment is an incomplete capital decision.
  • Reconciliation under regulatory scrutiny reinforced that a number is only as good as the control behind it.
  • Recommendation: layer scenario-based liquidity stress testing onto the daily monitor so the threshold becomes forward-looking — flagging a projected breach days ahead rather than reporting it after the fact.
